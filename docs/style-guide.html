Gourd WarsStyle guide

Plain flat vector. Solid shapes, one hard-edged shadow side, rounded forms, and no outlines or shine. The world is calm and the players and gourds are loud.

Decided November 9, 2026. Everything here is a mockup drawn in the browser. The game's code hasn't changed yet.

The rules

DoSolid flat shapes.

Every shape is one color. Detail comes from more shapes, like a pumpkin's darker side lobes, not from lines.

DoOne hard shadow side.

Light comes from the top left. Each player, gourd and hill gets a darker copy of itself on the bottom-right edge.

DoRounded forms.

Round limb ends, round heads, lobed pumpkins, soft hills and clouds.

DoCalm world, loud action.

Map colors stay softer than players, gourds and blasts, so the fight always reads first.

Don'tNo outlines and no shine.

No ink lines around shapes. No glossy highlights on gourds or heads.

Don'tNo cartoony extras.

No googly eyes, faces on pumpkins, party hats or KABOOM text. The goofiness comes from the physics.

Don'tNo gradients.

Skies that change color use flat bands instead.

DoHUD text on paper.

Text over the game sits on a paper chip or gets a paper outline, so it reads on every map.

Main palette: Pumpkin & Bark

Used for the logo, menus and HUD. It never changes from map to map. Click a color to copy its hex.

Players and effects

These colors are the same on every map. Back limbs are the player color mixed 18% toward black. Eyes are white with a dark pupil, and white X's when stunned.

Blast colors

A white flash ring at 80%, then flat puffs that pop out, cool from yellow to orange, and turn into the map's smoke color as they drift up.

Two-tone shading

How it's made

  • Light from the top left. The shadow side is bottom right.
  • Shadow shape: the silhouette minus a copy of it shifted toward the light.
  • Shadow color: multiply with the map's shade tint, so shadows take on the map's mood. Each map lists its tint below.
  • Shift: 0.04 m for players, 20% of the radius for gourds, 0.16 m sideways only for hills.
  • Contact shadow under anyone standing: a flat ellipse in the map's shadow color.

Maps

Ten maps, each with its own palette. The chips list sky, far and near layers, ground, platform top and the shade tint.

Gourds

Each gourd has a body color and a darker rib color, which is used for side lobes and details. Four changed in this round and are marked New.
